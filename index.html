Tianshu Wu

I am a Ph.D. student at Peking University, advised by Prof. Hao Dong. Before this, I earned my Bachelor's degree from the Harbin Institute of Technology.

My current research is centered on humanoid robot learning, with a specific focus on loco-manipulation and humanoid-object interaction. I am dedicated to developing general, generalizable, and scalable learning methods, aiming to empower humanoid robots to execute a wide variety of real-world tasks with human-like capability.
